Half-Life 1.5 and Counter-Strike 1.7
====================================

Half-Life 1.5 and Counter-Strike 1.7 are an unofficial set made by fans:
the Xash3D FWGS engine with Half-Life and Counter-Strike game libraries.
They are not made, endorsed or supported by Valve Corporation, and need
your own Steam copy of Half-Life (and of Counter-Strike for Counter-Strike
1.7). Half-Life and Counter-Strike are trademarks of Valve Corporation.

This installer puts together free software and copies the game data of
Half-Life and Counter-Strike from your own Steam installation.

Xash3D FWGS engine (xash3d.exe, xash.dll, ref_vk.dll, ref_gl.dll,
filesystem_stdio.dll, menu.dll, vgui2_support.dll)
  GNU General Public License, version 3 or later.
  https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html
  Source: https://github.com/aluzed/xash3d-fwgs

Half-Life game libraries (valve\cl_dlls\client_amd64.dll, valve\dlls\hl_amd64.dll)
  hlsdk-portable, under the Half-Life 1 SDK license of Valve Corporation:
  free for non-commercial use with Half-Life.
  Source: https://github.com/FWGS/hlsdk-portable

Counter-Strike game libraries (cstrike\cl_dlls\client_amd64.dll,
cstrike\cl_dlls\menu_amd64.dll, cstrike\extras.pk3)
  CS16Client, GNU General Public License, version 3 or later.
  Source: https://github.com/aluzed/cs16-client
cstrike\dlls\mp_amd64.dll
  ReGameDLL_CS, GNU General Public License, version 3 or later.
  Source: https://github.com/Velaron/ReGameDLL_CS
cstrike\dlls\yapb_amd64.dll
  YaPB, MIT License. Source: https://github.com/yapb/yapb

SDL2.dll
  zlib License. https://www.libsdl.org

vcruntime140.dll, vcruntime140_1.dll, msvcp140.dll
  Microsoft Visual C++ runtime, redistributed under the Visual Studio
  license terms.

  The valve and cstrike folders are copied from your own Steam installation

  Corporation and are covered by the Steam Subscriber Agreement. Nothing of
  them is included in this installer.
